# 👋 About Me

I am a **Master of Data Science student at the University of Houston-Clear Lake** with a **BBA in Marketing from Loyola University Chicago**. My academic and professional background combines marketing, analytics, and data science, giving me a multidisciplinary perspective on how data can be used to understand customers, evaluate performance, and solve business problems.

My professional experience spans digital marketing, marketing analytics, paid advertising, marketing automation, content strategy, and campaign management. These experiences sparked my interest in the analytical side of marketing—understanding not only what happened, but also why it happened and how data can support better decisions.

As I transitioned into data science, I developed a stronger technical foundation in **Python, SQL, statistics, machine learning, data visualization, and business analytics**. I enjoy working across the data lifecycle, from data cleaning and exploration to modeling, evaluation, visualization, and communicating actionable insights**.

My academic and personal projects have allowed me to apply these skills to real-world business problems, including **marketing campaign prediction, financial analytics, M&A analysis, and large-scale data visualization**. I have worked with tools and techniques such as **Python, pandas, scikit-learn, LightGBM, Tableau, statistical analysis, regression, and classification models.

I am particularly interested in using data to understand customer behavior, marketing performance, business trends, and growth opportunities. I enjoy translating complex datasets into insights that are clear, practical, and relevant to business decision-making.
